# Design direction — 20 years of you

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Midnight Envelope
Very dark, cinematic, and intimate, with a reveal that feels like opening a letter at 12:00 AM.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Sea Glass Editorial
Warm ivory, ink, sea-glass blue, and tactile film texture arranged like a personal art-book with quiet motion.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Sunlit Contact Sheet
A brighter photographic direction inspired by contact sheets, handwritten notes, and a summer afternoon archive.
Probability: 0.03

## Chosen approach: Sea Glass Editorial

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design with analog photography, Swiss-informed typography, and the tactile restraint of a small-run art book.

### Core Principles
1. Let the photography and words breathe; avoid decorative birthday clichés.
2. Use asymmetry and chapter markers to make the page feel authored rather than templated.
3. Reserve motion for meaningful transitions: reveal, lift, unfold, and progress.
4. Keep warmth in the copy while letting the visual system stay composed and premium.

### Color Philosophy
Warm ivory is the paper of the keepsake. Ink charcoal carries the serious and intimate moments. Sea-glass blue is the ownable accent: calm, youthful, and drawn from the supplied photographs rather than imposed on them. A muted clay note appears sparingly as a human counterpoint, never as a loud party color.

### Layout Paradigm
A vertical digital art-book. Each chapter is a full-bleed or near-full-bleed scene with a small editorial index on the edge, offset text blocks, and occasional horizontal rails. Content enters through the scroll instead of being placed into a repeated card grid.

### Signature Elements
- Small chapter index labels such as `03 / 06` and vertical rules.
- Cream paper panels over ink sections, with an offset sea-glass edge.
- Photo cards that lift, rotate, and reveal the next image like a tactile stack.

### Interaction Philosophy
The interface should reward curiosity without withholding basic navigation. Every action gets a small, legible response: a button leans, a card lifts, a wish advances, a candle dims. Nothing depends on hover and nothing traps the visitor.

### Animation
Use 180–700ms opacity and transform transitions, with slower 900ms chapter reveals only for major moments. Stagger text by 40–70ms. Use a 1–2% slow photo drift in hero scenes. Disable non-essential movement under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

### Typography System
Display: `DM Serif Display`, used for emotional statements and large numerals. Body/UI: `Manrope`, used for readable copy, labels, and controls. Italic display moments are used as a handwritten-feeling accent without adding a third typeface.

### Brand Essence
A private digital keepsake for Aishwarya, made by a friend who sees her as family, designed to be opened slowly on her twentieth birthday. Personality: **observant, playful, sincere**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are short, specific, and a little self-aware. CTAs sound like a friend nudging her forward, never like a campaign. Microcopy can tease, then soften.

Example lines:
- “Apparently you’re an adult now. I’m not convinced.”
- “Take your time. I made this to be opened slowly.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact interlocking loop mark that hints at a folded ribbon, a north star, and the shape of `20` without spelling the name. The mark is used as a small seal, favicon, and chapter punctuation.

### Signature Brand Color
**Sea-glass blue `#8FB8B3`** — a calm, ownable accent that carries the memory-archive feeling across ink and ivory surfaces.
